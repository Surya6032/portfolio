Selected work

The platform grew out of a real problem: multiple teams were maintaining their own copies of the same source data, each with slightly different logic. The goal was to consolidate that into a Bronze/Silver/Gold lakehouse where the transformation rules lived once and the outputs could be trusted by analytics, reporting, and data science downstream.

  • Defined clear layer contracts — raw ingestion, conformed schemas, business-ready gold tables — so teams knew exactly what to expect at each stage.
  • Used Delta Change Data Feed throughout to avoid full-table reprocessing; incremental by default.
  • Standardized ingestion patterns across 10+ upstream systems — new sources now follow a documented template rather than being built from scratch.
  • Cut time-to-insight for analytics and reporting teams by 50%.
Problem
The batch pipeline completed around 2am. By the time finance opened their dashboards, the data was already 6–8 hours old and decisions were being made on stale numbers.
Approach
Replaced the full-refresh logic with Delta CDF-based incremental processing and Spark Structured Streaming. Ran a parallel validation period before decommissioning the old job.
  • Eliminated full-table scans — only changed records flow through on each run.
  • Added monitoring, retry logic, and alerting so the new pipeline is easier to operate than the old one.
  • Reporting latency went from 24 hours to under 15 minutes.

The trigger was a pattern of incidents where downstream teams would discover data problems hours after the fact. Schema drift, late-arriving data, sudden volume drops — all of them surfacing in dashboards rather than at the pipeline boundary where they could be caught early.

I implemented Great Expectations checks and anomaly detection monitors at each medallion layer, integrated quality gates into CI/CD so bad data can't be deployed, and built freshness and volume monitors that alert before consumers notice an issue. Data incidents dropped 35%. More importantly, teams stopped treating shared datasets as unreliable.
